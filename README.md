## About

I investigate how language and multimodal models reason, where current evaluations break down, and how rigorous, verifiable benchmarks can reveal their limitations.

My background combines mathematics, computer science, AI/ML engineering, and mathematics education. My recent work has focused on designing and hand-validating original mathematical and scientific multimodal evaluation tasks, with complete reference solutions and explicit evaluation criteria.

I am especially interested in difficult problems where success requires auditable, multi-step reasoning rather than surface-level pattern matching.

## Research interests

- LLM evaluation, benchmark design, and evaluation validity
- Mathematical reasoning and olympiad-style problem solving
- Scientific and multimodal reasoning
- Model failure analysis and benchmark robustness
- Multilingual NLP and low-resource African languages
- Trustworthy AI for education and underserved communities
